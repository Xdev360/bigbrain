Context

From MVP prototype to a production app

The brief: a web platform where users could follow live crypto markets, fund an account, and get support, with an admin team verifying deposits behind the scenes. I designed every screen and built the frontend in Next.js on Supabase, with Binance's API providing live prices.

Key decisions

Three problems worth solving well

01

Prices that feel alive, but calm

Prices update constantly, so numbers use fixed-width digits and tint green or red only for a moment when they change. Traders can scan the list without it jittering.

Rejected: full-row colour flashes. Exciting in a demo, exhausting after an hour.

02

Deposits as a tracked process

Money in is the most anxious moment for a user. Each deposit shows its steps and who it's waiting on (you or us), so "where's my money" has an answer on screen.

Rejected: a single "Processing" status. It leaves people guessing and sends them to support.
