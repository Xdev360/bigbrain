I'm Oladele. I design products and write the frontend that ships them. I started building businesses at sixteen, and most of them failed. That taught me more than any course did: a product lives or dies on whether people understand it.

Today I focus on crypto products, where that's truer than anywhere else. Every signature is permanent, every fee is real money, and a confusing screen costs people more than time.

I also run Wintech Studio, a small Lagos studio building websites, products and AI automation for businesses. It's where I learned to work directly with founders and ship every week.

This site is the crypto work. Everything else, brand, websites and client projects, lives at xbigbrainnx.xyz.
